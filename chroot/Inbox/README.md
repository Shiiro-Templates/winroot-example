# Inbox

A bounded intake area for material whose final semantic home has not yet been
decided.

- `Downloads`: downloads initiated by the user.
- `Imports`: staged material imported from devices, archives, or other systems.
- `Drop`: quick cross-device or inter-application handoff.

Nothing should live here permanently. Review items and either delete them or
promote them to `Home`, `Workspaces`, `Software`, or `Archive`. Machine-managed
redownloadable artifacts belong in `../Cache/Downloads`.
